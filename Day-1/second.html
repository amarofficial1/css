Bioluminescent bays are some of the most magical natural wonders on Earth. In these unique coastal waters, millions of microscopic organisms called dinoflagellates glow with a bright blue light whenever the water is disturbed. When a fish swims by, a kayaker paddles an oar, or a wave rolls onto the shore, the water sparkles like a field of underwater stars. Famous spots like Mosquito Bay in Puerto Rico draw travelers from all over the world who want to witness this glowing neon magic in person.
